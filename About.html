About

Welcome to Opinionated, a community-driven music review platform where every opinion matters. Opinionated is a place for music lovers to rate and review their favorite artists, albums, and songs. Whether you're sharing your thoughts on a new release, recommending a hidden gem, or discovering what others are listening to, Opinionated helps bring music fans together. Our mission is to create a space where people can express their opinions respectfully, discover new music, and explore a wide variety of tastes and genres.

What You Can Do

⭐ Rate artists, albums, and songs
📝 Read and write music reviews
🎤 Discover new music through the community
👤 Create a profile and track your reviews
❤️ Share your opinions with other music fans

Our Community

Music means something different to everyone, and that's what makes it exciting. We encourage honest, respectful reviews and welcome all perspectives. Whether you love pop, rock, hip-hop, jazz, country, or classical music, there's a place for your voice on Opinionated. Thank you for visiting Opinionated. Explore, discover, and don't be afraid to be opinionated!

# Website Project Context I am building a website for a website-building camp. The website is called **Opinionated** and it is a public music rating and review platform. **Live Website:** https://macwillsie.github.io/opinionated When helping me, you can reference the live website to see my current progress and suggest improvements based on the existing design and layout. ## Overall Concept Opinionated is a community-driven website where users can: * Rate artists, albums, and songs * Write reviews * Read reviews from other users * Discover new music * Create a profile and keep track of their reviews and favorites The website is inspired by platforms like Letterboxd, Rate Your Music, IMDb, and Spotify, but it focuses entirely on music. ## Style & Theme I want the website to feel modern, clean, and professional. Design goals: * Dark mode * Rounded corners * Cards instead of plain lists * Smooth hover animations * Modern buttons * Music-themed icons * Lots of spacing (not cluttered) I want it to look like a real website, not a beginner HTML project. ## Website Pages ### 1. Home Contains: * Hero banner * Search bar * Trending This Week * Featured Review * Latest Reviews * Top Rated Artists * New Releases * Community Poll * Footer ### 2. Reviews Shows all community reviews. Each review is displayed as a card containing: * Album cover or artist image * Star rating * Artist * Album or song * Username * Date * Review text * Like count * Comment button * Read More button There are filters at the top: * All * Artists * Albums * Songs There is also: * Search bar * Sort by Newest * Highest Rated * Most Popular ### 3. Artists Displays artists with: * Artist image * Average rating * Genre * Number of reviews * View Artist button ### 4. Profile Instead of having a separate "Write a Review" page, there is a sidebar button that opens the review form. The Profile page includes: * Profile picture * Username * Bio * Stats * Favorite artists * Favorite albums * Favorite songs * Recent reviews * Edit Profile button Example stats: * Reviews Posted * Average Rating * Favorite Genre * Favorite Artist * Helpful Votes * Followers * Following * Member Since ### 5. About Explains: * What Opinionated is * The goal of the community * What users can do * Community guidelines ## Navigation The navigation contains: * Home * Reviews * Artists * Profile * About There is also a sidebar with: * Write a Review * Search ## Design Style Whenever suggesting layouts, prefer: * Cards * Sections * Rounded containers * Hover effects * Shadows * Images * Icons * Modern spacing Avoid plain text lists whenever possible. ## Help I Want Whenever I ask for help, I'd like suggestions that improve the design and make it feel like a real, modern website.
